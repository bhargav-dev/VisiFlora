What Are The Ingredients In VisiFlora?

VisiFlora is formulated with 8 named ingredients — including Vitamin A (as beta-carotene), Vitamin C, Vitamin E, Zinc, Lutein — each listed individually below with the role it plays in the formula, so you can see exactly what you are taking before you buy.

  • 1

    Vitamin A (as beta-carotene)

    CORE VISION NUTRIENT

    A well-known nutrient for maintaining normal vision, particularly for the eyes' ability to adapt to light and dark and to keep the surface of the eye healthy.

  • 2

    Vitamin C

    ANTIOXIDANT SUPPORT

    A water-soluble antioxidant that helps protect eye tissue from everyday oxidative stress and supports the collagen structures that make up parts of the eye.

  • 3

    Vitamin E

    ANTIOXIDANT PROTECTION

    A fat-soluble antioxidant that works alongside vitamin C to defend delicate eye cells from free-radical damage linked to aging and environmental exposure.

  • 4

    Zinc

    TRACE MINERAL FOR EYE FUNCTION

    A mineral concentrated in the retina that supports normal visual function and helps transport vitamin A to the eye where it is needed.

  • 5

    Lutein

    MACULAR PIGMENT CAROTENOID

    A carotenoid that concentrates in the macula, helping filter blue light and supporting the long-term health of the central retina.

  • 6

    Zeaxanthin

    MACULAR PIGMENT CAROTENOID

    Lutein's partner carotenoid, found in high concentrations in the macula, where it contributes to light filtering and antioxidant defense.

  • 7

    Bilberry extract

    BOTANICAL EYE WELLNESS SUPPORT

    A berry extract traditionally used in eye-focused formulas for its anthocyanin content and its role in supporting comfortable, well-nourished eyes.

  • 8

    Grape seed extract

    POLYPHENOL ANTIOXIDANT

    Rich in oligomeric proanthocyanidins, it adds antioxidant support that helps protect the small blood vessels and connective tissue around the eyes.

VisiFlora FAQ, Answered Honestly

  • What is VisiFlora actually for?
    VisiFlora is an eye wellness supplement designed to support everyday eye care and the maintenance of healthy vision. It is a daily nutritional aid, not a treatment for any eye disease or a replacement for an eye exam.
  • What is inside VisiFlora?
    The formula centers on eye-focused nutrients and botanicals: vitamin A (as beta-carotene), vitamin C, vitamin E, zinc, lutein, zeaxanthin, bilberry extract and grape seed extract.
  • How do I take it?
    Take 1 capsule daily with water, ideally with a meal. One bottle of 60 capsules gives you a two-month supply at that serving.
  • How long before I notice anything?
    Eye nutrients work gradually. Many people treat VisiFlora as a long-term habit and look at the 60–90 day mark rather than expecting an overnight change. Results vary from person to person.
  • Is VisiFlora safe, and are there side effects?
    It contains common, well-studied nutrients and botanicals at typical supplemental amounts. Some people may notice mild digestive upset if taken on an empty stomach. If you take prescription medication, have a medical condition, or are pregnant or nursing, check with your doctor first.

    Anyone under 18, people with a diagnosed eye condition who have not consulted their eye doctor, and anyone taking blood thinners or other interacting medication should speak to a healthcare professional before use. Smokers should be cautious with high-dose beta-carotene and ask a doctor first.
